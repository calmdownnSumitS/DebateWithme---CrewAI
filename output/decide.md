Because the motion rests on a historical inaccuracy and the pro side’s own arguments describe the belief as a pattern‑seeking, scientifically baseless cultural phenomenon rather than a sincere, pre‑existing prediction, the argument against the motion provides the more convincing and substantively grounded case.
